THE PROBLEM

Resumes say a lot. They prove very little.

StudentsDon't know which opportunities are genuine, or which skills companies actually require right now.
CollegesLack accurate, verifiable data on how employable their students really are.
CompaniesStruggle to identify capable candidates from resumes full of self-declared skills.
Smaller collegesOften have unequal access to industry opportunities and hiring pipelines.
HOW IT WORKS

One verified pipeline, from classroom to offer letter

Six steps. No step can be skipped or faked — each one hands the next verifiable proof, not a claim.

Create profile

Submit evidence

College verifies

Gaps surface

Roles match

Outcome tracked

College and company follow the hire through to completion.

HOW VERIFICATION WORKS

Not every proof carries the same weight — and it shouldn't

🥉 SELF-DECLARED

Project link added

Student attaches a repo, deployment or write-up. Visible to recruiters, but flagged as unverified.

🥈 FACULTY VERIFIED

Internal review done

A faculty member or TPO has reviewed the code or output and signed off on the claim.

🥇 INDUSTRY VERIFIED

Third-party assessment

Backed by an external assessment score, certification, or real internship proof.

Every verification carries a visible audit trail — timestamp, reviewer ID and department — so recruiters can trust what they're reading.
