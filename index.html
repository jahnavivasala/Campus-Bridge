ABOUT CAMPUSBRIDGE

A bridge between verified skills and genuine opportunities.

CampusBridge is designed to bring students, colleges and companies onto one transparent platform where skills can be demonstrated, verified and connected to relevant opportunities.

What we solve

  • Self-declared skills are difficult to verify
  • Students struggle to identify genuine opportunities
  • Colleges need clearer employability data
  • Companies need stronger signals than resume keywords

What makes it different

  • Evidence-backed student skill profiles
  • College verification of submitted evidence
  • Transparent opportunity matching
  • Clear skill gaps for target roles

Who uses it

  • Students building verified Skill Passports
  • Colleges and TPOs managing employability
  • Companies searching for suitable candidates
  • Admins monitoring platform trust and safety
THE PROBLEM

Resumes say a lot. They prove very little.

Students Don't know which opportunities are genuine, or which skills companies actually require right now.
Colleges Lack accurate, verifiable data on how employable their students really are.
Companies Struggle to identify capable candidates from resumes full of self-declared skills.
Smaller colleges Often have unequal access to industry opportunities and hiring pipelines.
HOW IT WORKS

One verified pipeline, from classroom to offer letter

Create profile

Student declares skills and academic details.

Submit evidence

Projects, assessments and certifications back every skill.

College verifies

Faculty or TPO reviews and approves the evidence.

Gaps surface

Platform shows exactly what's missing for target roles.

Roles match

Companies post structured openings; the engine matches eligible students.

Outcome tracked

College and company follow the hire through to completion.
